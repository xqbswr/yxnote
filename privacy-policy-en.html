Book Reader Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-22 ｜ Last updated: 2026-10-30

In short: Book Reader has no account system and does not collect your name, phone number, email or any other personal identifier. Your books, progress and settings stay on your own device. To improve the app and diagnose crashes, anonymous usage statistics and crash logs are sent to Google Firebase (never your search terms, book titles or reading content). Ads are served by Google AdMob.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

Book Reader has no registration or login, requests no name, phone number, email, contacts or location, and asks for no data that could identify you. Anonymous usage statistics and crash diagnostics are sent to Google Firebase, as described in 1.1 and 1.2.

1.1 Advertising & Advertising Identifier

This app displays ads via Google AdMob to support free operation. Ads are served directly by Google:

  • Google may use the Advertising ID and other device information to serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure performance, governed by the Google Ads Policies and Google Privacy Policy.
  • We (the developer) cannot access or store your advertising identifier or ad-interaction data; it is handled directly by Google.
  • In the EEA, UK and other applicable regions, we show Google's consent form before serving personalized ads.
  • You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings.

1.2 Usage Analytics & Crash Diagnostics (Google Firebase)

The app uses two Google Firebase services to understand feature usage and diagnose failures in the field. All of this data is anonymous and cannot be linked to your identity.

Firebase Analytics records:

  • Screen changes (library, sources, rankings, reader, settings, etc.)
  • Number of book-source searches and how many results they returned (never the search terms you type)
  • Actions such as adding a book to the shelf, opening a book, and ad display or load failures
  • App version, device model, OS version, interface language, and a coarse region inferred from your IP address

Firebase Crashlytics records crash stack traces, the app state at the time of the crash, device model and OS version — used solely to locate and fix problems.

Details:

  • Each analytics event is associated with a randomly generated app instance ID, which is unrelated to your identity. We do not collect your name, phone number, email or account, and we do not upload book titles, book-source URLs or reading content.
  • This data is processed by Google as our data processor and stored on servers located outside your country, governed by Firebase Privacy and Security and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Uninstalling the app stops all reporting. To request deletion assistance, contact us at the address in section 7.

2. Where Data Is Stored

The following data is stored locally on your device (in the app sandbox) and is deleted entirely when you uninstall:

  • Books you import (TXT / EPUB files)
  • Book metadata and cover cache
  • Reading progress, font/theme and other reading settings
  • Online book-source configurations

3. Network Requests

Only when you use the online book-source search/reading feature does the app send network requests — directly to the book-source websites you configured, to fetch chapter lists and content. Please note:

  • Requests go directly from your device to those websites. They never pass through our servers (we have none).
  • Book-source websites will see your IP address and other standard network information, and handle it under their own privacy policies.
  • We cannot control third-party book-source websites or their content. Use your own judgment when adding sources.

If you only import local files (TXT / EPUB), the app makes no network requests at all.

4. Permissions

The app requests a single system permission: INTERNET, solely for the online book-source feature described above. We request no storage, location, camera, microphone, or contacts permissions.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. It collects no names or contact details, and the analytics data described in 1.2 cannot be linked to any individual.

6. Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
